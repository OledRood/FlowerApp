Project Name: Flower Tracker
Category: Mobile Application (iOS & Android)
Purpose: To help users efficiently manage and care for their plants by tracking watering schedules, growth progress, and other essential details.

Key Features:

Plant Profiles:
Create individual profiles for each plant, including its name, species, age, and planting date.
Add photos to document growth over time.
Watering Reminders:
Set customizable watering schedules based on each plant's needs.
Receive notifications when it’s time to water.
Growth Tracking:
Log the plant’s height, number of leaves, and other growth metrics.
Visualize progress with charts and timelines.
Care Tips:
Access a database of plant care tips tailored to each species.
Get reminders for fertilizing, pruning, and repotting.
Photo Journal:
Capture and store photos of your plants to track their development.
Organize photos by date or growth stage.
Community Sharing:
Share your plant progress with friends or a community of plant enthusiasts.
Get advice and inspiration from other users.
Offline Mode:
Use the app without an internet connection, with data syncing once reconnected.
Target Audience:

Plant lovers, gardeners, and anyone who wants to keep their plants healthy and thriving.
Beginners looking for guidance on plant care.
Technology Stack:

Frontend: React Native (for cross-platform compatibility).
Backend: Firebase (for real-time data storage and synchronization).
Database: Firestore (for storing user data and plant profiles).
Additional Tools: Push notifications, image storage, and analytics.
Why This App?

The Flower Tracker app simplifies plant care by providing a centralized platform to monitor and manage all aspects of plant health. It’s designed to be user-friendly, visually appealing, and highly functional, making it a must-have tool for both casual plant owners and serious gardeners.

Tagline: "Grow your plants smarter, not harder!"
